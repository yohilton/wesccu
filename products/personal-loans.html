Loan options

Personal loans for different needs

Explore WESCCU loan options, key terms, savings requirements, and supporting documents. Confirm which terms apply to your application with the team.

Short Term Loan (STL)

For members’ business operations or personal financial needs.

Interest
3% per month
Term
Up to 12 months; flexible repayment
Savings
At least 6 months of saving and savings equal to at least 50% of the requested loan
Processing fee
0.5%
Requirements and business documents
  • Fully paid minimum shares and completed application form
  • Two passport-sized photographs and address verification
  • Foreign applicants: residence permit
  • Post-dated cheques covering principal and interest repayments
  • Guarantor
  • For business applications: registration documents; board resolution for companies; six months of business activity records; and constitution for unincorporated groups or associations

Long Term Loan (LTL)

For members’ longer-term business or personal financial needs.

Interest
3% per month
Term
13–24 months; flexible repayment
Amount
Up to twice the balance in the member’s savings account
Savings
At least 6 months of saving and savings equal to at least 50% of the requested loan
Processing fee
0.5%
Requirements and business documents
  • Fully paid minimum shares and completed application form
  • Two passport-sized photographs and address verification
  • Foreign applicants: residence permit
  • Post-dated cheques covering principal and interest repayments
  • Guarantor
  • For business applications: registration documents; board resolution for companies; six months of business activity records; and constitution for unincorporated groups or associations

Bridge Loan (BGL)

Short-notice funds for members without reducing their savings.

Interest
1.45% per month
Term
Up to 36 months; flexible repayment
Amount
Up to 80% of the member’s savings
Disbursement
Within 24 hours
Processing fee
0.5%
Requirements and business documents
  • Fully paid minimum shares and completed application form
  • Two passport-sized photographs; address verification and digital address
  • Foreign applicants: residence permit
  • Post-dated cheques covering principal and interest repayments
  • For business applications: registration documents; board resolution for companies; six months of business activity records; and constitution for unincorporated groups or associations

Personal Relief Loan (PRL)

For self-employed and salaried members needing help with assets, bills, or other immediate expenses.

Maximum
GHS 5,000
Interest
3.5% per month
Term
Up to 6 months; flexible repayment
Fees
1% flat; a 0.5% processing fee is also listed under requirements
Savings
Minimum savings balance and at least 10% of the amount requested in savings
Requirements and security
  • Fully paid minimum shares and completed application form
  • Two passport-sized photographs; address verification and digital address
  • Foreign applicants: residence permit
  • Post-dated cheques covering principal and interest repayments
  • Collateral; a member in good standing or a relative may act as guarantor
  • The product information notes that members may access a facility immediately after joining; the listed savings, shares, and application requirements still apply

Staff and Officers Loan

For WESCCU staff and committee officers with personal financial needs.

Maximum
GHS 20,000
Interest
1% per month
Term
Up to 24 months; flexible repayment
Debt-to-income
Staff ratio must not exceed 40%
Processing fee
0.5%
Requirements
  • Fully paid minimum shares and minimum savings balance
  • Staff must be confirmed and have worked with WESCCU for at least one year
  • Completed application form; two passport-sized photographs; address verification
  • Foreign applicants: residence permit
All listed loans include the CUA Risk Management Programme. WESCCU may not request documents already on file; only unavailable documents may be requested. Loans are subject to eligibility and approval. For the Personal Relief Loan, confirm the applicable processing fee with WESCCU before applying.
What to ask about

Understand your options before applying.

Talk to the credit union about eligibility and repayment obligations before deciding whether a loan is right for you.

Eligibility

Confirm membership, savings, documentation, and approval requirements.

Repayment terms

Ask about repayment periods, instalments, and any fees.

Loan costs

Request the current rate and total cost before making a commitment.

Questions to ask before applying

What will I need to apply?

Ask WESCCU for its current eligibility and document checklist, including any membership or savings conditions.

How much will repayment cost?

Request the full repayment schedule, interest calculation, fees, and total amount payable. Choose a repayment amount that fits your budget.

Can I repay early?

Ask whether early or additional payments are permitted and whether charges or adjustments apply.

Loans are subject to eligibility, assessment, and WESCCU approval. Confirm current rates, fees, and repayment terms with the team.
