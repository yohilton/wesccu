Make your advisory enquiry specific

What topics could I bring to a discussion?

You might ask about bookkeeping routines, cash-flow planning, pricing, stock management, or planning a business goal. Confirm which topics WESCCU currently supports.

What should I prepare?

Bring a short description of your business, the question you want help with, and any records that would make the discussion clearer.

Is advisory support included with another service?

Ask whether the guidance is available to all members, connected to a financing product, or subject to a separate fee.

The scope and availability of advisory support should be confirmed directly with WESCCU.
