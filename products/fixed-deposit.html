Questions to ask before placing a deposit

How will I know what my deposit earns?

Ask for a written illustration showing the rate, term, calculation method, applicable fees, and expected maturity amount.

Can I withdraw before the end of the term?

Confirm whether early withdrawal is allowed and what rate adjustment, fee, or notice period may apply.

What happens at maturity?

Ask whether the funds are paid to another account, renewed automatically, or require an instruction from you.

Rates, terms, eligibility, and early withdrawal conditions are subject to WESCCU’s current offer. Confirm details with the team.
