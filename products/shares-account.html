Questions to ask about membership shares

Are these shares the same as buying shares on a stock exchange?

No. These are cooperative membership shares in WESCCU, not publicly traded securities. Ask the team to explain what ownership means under the credit union’s rules.

What rights come with membership?

Request the current membership rules and ask how voting, participation, and any member distributions are determined.

Can I withdraw or transfer my shares?

Ask about the rules, timing, and conditions for withdrawing, transferring, or redeeming shares.

Share terms, membership requirements, and any returns are subject to WESCCU’s rules and current terms. Ask the team for confirmed details.
