Financing for business and projects

Explore WESCCU financing options, eligibility criteria, repayment terms, and supporting documents.

Business Relief Loan (BRL)

Working capital for businesses facing critical, pressing operational needs.

Eligible businesses
Sole proprietorships, partnerships, and limited liability companies
Maximum
GHS 10,000
Interest
3.5% per month
Term
Up to 6 months; flexible repayment
Fees
1% flat; a 0.5% processing fee is also listed under requirements
Savings
Minimum savings balance and at least 10% of the loan amount in savings
Requirements
  • Fully paid minimum shares and completed application form
  • Business registration documents; constitution for unincorporated societies and enterprises; six months of business activity records
  • Two passport-sized photographs of shareholders or signatories
  • Address verification and digital address; foreign applicants need a residence permit
  • Post-dated cheques covering principal and interest repayments
  • Collateral and a guarantor who is a member in good standing or a relative
  • Companies also need a board resolution

Project Financing Loan (PFL)

For individual and institutional members raising capital to pay for or support projects.

Interest
3.5% per month
Term
Tied to project inflows, up to 36 months
Repayment
May be repaid before the term ends without penalty
Fees
1% flat; a 0.5% processing fee is also listed under requirements
Savings
At least 6 months of saving and at least 10% of the loan amount in savings
Requirements
  • Fully paid minimum shares and completed application form
  • Two passport-sized photographs and address verification
  • Foreign applicants: residence permit
  • Post-dated cheques covering principal and interest repayments
  • Collateral and guarantor
  • For business applications: registration documents; board resolution for companies; six months of business activity records; and constitution for unincorporated societies and enterprises

Contract Financing Loan (CFL)

Working capital for construction companies and vendors of blue-chip companies while they await payment on pre-approved contracts.

Eligible businesses
Construction firms and vendors of blue-chip companies
Facility
Up to 70% of expected contract payment
Interest
2.5% per month
Term
Up to 6 months; flexible repayment
Fees
1% flat; a 0.5% processing fee is also listed under requirements
Savings
Minimum savings balance and at least 5% of the loan amount in savings
Requirements
  • Fully paid minimum shares and completed application form
  • Assignment of receivables from the contract employer
  • Business registration documents and six months of business activity records
  • Two passport-sized photographs of shareholders or signatories
  • Address verification and digital address; foreign applicants need a residence permit
  • Post-dated cheques covering principal and interest repayments; collateral
  • Companies also need a board resolution
All listed loans include the CUA Risk Management Programme. WESCCU may not request documents already on file; only unavailable documents may be requested. Loans are subject to eligibility and approval. For products showing two processing-fee figures, confirm the applicable fee with WESCCU before applying.

Questions to ask about business finance

What business costs might I discuss?

You can explain whether you need working capital, stock, equipment, project costs, or support to expand. WESCCU can confirm which needs fit its current products.

What information should I prepare?

It may help to bring a clear use-of-funds summary, basic business records, cash-flow estimates, and any relevant contracts or invoices. Ask for WESCCU’s exact checklist.

Does a contract automatically qualify for finance?

No. A contract may support an application, but eligibility, repayment ability, documentation, and approval still need to be assessed by WESCCU.

Financing is subject to assessment, eligibility, and WESCCU approval. No specific amount, rate, or timeline is guaranteed; confirm current terms with the team.
