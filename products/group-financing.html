WESCCU invites organized groups, associations, and community-based enterprises to discuss their financing needs with the team.

Enquire about Group Financing
Loan option

Group Loan (GRL)

Designed to help groups such as churches, trade associations, unions, and company staff groups access financing for their members. Facilities are booked in individual members’ names, and group members support one another with repayment.

Features and terms

Maximum facility
GHS 20,000
Interest
3.5% per month
Repayment
Flexible weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly payments in line with group arrangements
Term
Initial loan up to 6 months; repeat loans may run up to 12 months
Fees
1% flat; a 0.5% processing fee is also listed under requirements
Savings
The group must save at least 10% of the amount requested, and each beneficiary must also hold at least 10% of the amount requested in their individual savings account
Security
Group provides general security for individual members; collateral is negotiable
Group and member requirements
  • The group must hold an account with WESCCU
  • Each beneficiary must be a member of the applying group, in good standing with their organization and WESCCU
  • Completed application with names and details of all beneficiaries, plus the group constitution
  • Two passport-sized photographs and address verification for each beneficiary
  • Foreign applicants: residence permit
  • Guarantors must be members of the group
  • Regular meetings between WESCCU officers and group members
The product information lists both a 1% flat processing fee and a 0.5% processing fee. Confirm the applicable fee with WESCCU before applying. This loan includes the CUA Risk Management Programme. WESCCU may not request documents already on file; only unavailable documents may be requested.
What to ask about

Start with your group’s plans.

Contact WESCCU to discuss your group structure, the purpose of financing, and the steps needed for an assessment.

Group eligibility

Confirm which types of groups may apply and any membership conditions.

Application steps

Ask about group records, member details, and supporting documents.

Terms and repayment

Discuss assessment, repayment responsibilities, rates, and fees.

Questions for your group

Which groups can enquire?

Contact WESCCU with your group’s purpose, membership structure, and location. The team can confirm which groups are eligible under current rules.

Who is responsible for repayment?

Ask WESCCU to explain each member’s responsibilities, any group or individual obligations, and what happens if a payment is missed.

What should we bring to an initial discussion?

Prepare a summary of the group, the purpose and amount requested, expected repayment source, and any available records. Confirm the required documents with WESCCU.

Group financing depends on WESCCU’s current eligibility rules, assessment, and approval. Contact the team for confirmed details.
