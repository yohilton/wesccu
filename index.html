Our story

A cooperative built around local business

WESCCU grew from a practical idea: give people and businesses in the Western Region a cooperative way to save and access credit.

Established by the Sekondi-Takoradi Chamber of Commerce and Industry (STCCI), the credit union was shaped by the needs of the local business community. Members pool their savings and share capital, helping make financial services more rooted in the people they serve.

That purpose still guides the story—from WESCCU’s early growth in Takoradi to its 2015 expansion into Elubo, where traders and businesses needed access to credit close to the Ghana–Côte d’Ivoire border.

Growing with the community

  1. 2011

    A local idea takes shape

    STCCI establishes WESCCU to respond to financial needs in the business community.

  2. 2012

    Members build momentum

    WESCCU continued to grow its membership and strengthen its role in the local business community.

  3. 2015

    Extending service to Elubo

    WESCCU opens an Elubo office to support traders and businesses in the border community.

Historical sources: MyJoyOnline on WESCCU’s 2011 inception, GNA on the Elubo office opening.
